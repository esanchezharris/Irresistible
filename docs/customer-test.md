# 20-minute sales usability session

Use this with a B2B salesperson. Do not teach the interface before the first task.

## Setup

“This is a synthetic quoting tool. Imagine you are preparing a nonstandard enterprise quote. Please think out loud. I am testing the product, not you.”

## Tasks

1. Enter a deal you might plausibly sell: customer, users or seats, term, products or usage, budget, payment terms, and any nonstandard request.
2. Decide whether you trust the resulting year-one price. Show what you inspected.
3. Explain who must approve the deal and why.
4. Choose one proposed alternative you would actually take back to the buyer—or reject all of them.
5. Find one assumption you would change before sending the quote.

## Questions

- What did you expect to happen that did not happen?
- Which number or explanation did you trust least?
- Did the approval graph match how you think about getting a deal done?
- Which alternative felt useful versus artificial?
- What would make this safe enough to use before a customer call?
- If you could change one thing, what would it be?

## Observe

- Time to first successful prompt.
- Whether they inspect assumptions before price.
- Whether they distinguish parallel from sequential approvals.
- Whether the budget delta is understood without explanation.
- Whether they can explain an alternative’s tradeoff in their own words.
- Any field or term they naturally use that the parser misses.
